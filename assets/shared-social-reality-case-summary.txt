FROM PRIVATE AI RELATIONSHIPS TO SHARED SOCIAL REALITY

Question: Can separate private relationships with one artificial social actor shape subsequent human cooperation and shared understanding?

Research role: Independent project lead with Dr. Chen Zhou at Glasgow, developing a temporal extension of the lab's existing paradigm.

Current build: Prototype 01, a working scripted browser MVP developed with Codex assistance during the portfolio upgrade. Not an earlier independent engineering project or a lab-approved study system.

Implementation: Shared/separate character mapping; three turns of choice-based personalization; independent page-memory state; ordered events; local JSON export; public-metadata-only handoff; immediate/seven-day eligibility arithmetic and a labeled delayed-clock preview; illustrative public-goods payoff calculator.

Software evidence: Engine tests and verification report are included in the source repository. No recruited participants, usability findings, behavioral pilot, or confirmatory evidence exist for this implementation.

Proposed study: Independent three-person groups, shared/separate identity x immediate/seven-day first human meeting, AI absent during human interaction. Primary delayed contrast C-D; direct identity-by-delay comparison (C-D)-(A-B). Actual scheduling, private commitments, compensation, power planning, ethics and preregistration remain to be completed.

Limits: No LLM, authentication, research backend, saved follow-up, or live human-group discussion. Scripted identity continuity does not establish a felt relationship or a social transfer effect.

Next: Review/pilot comprehension and relational credibility, then develop approved study infrastructure and the most informative behavioral test.
