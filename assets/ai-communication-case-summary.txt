AI-Assisted Interpersonal Communication
University of Southern California
Analysis ongoing · Manuscript in preparation

Portfolio case summary; not a full manuscript.

Research Question
When does AI assistance become learning—and when does it become substitution?

Why It Matters
In conflict, apology, and difficult self-expression, AI can help people decide what to say. Whether that support also develops their own judgment and communicative agency is a separate question.

Study at a Glance
Two pilot studies (n = 55; n = 45) and a main study (n = 222) examine AI support in conflict, apology, emotional uncertainty, and difficult self-expression. The study sequence includes a pre-survey, chatbot interaction, and post-survey.

What I Built or Contributed
Conversational coding frameworks, an online guide, and a follow-up feedback survey; mixed-methods analyses are ongoing.

Methods & Analysis
I combine theory-driven coding of conversational and interview data with mixed-effects analyses of pre–post measures. Reading the interaction alongside the scores helps distinguish what the system does from how participants experience its assistance.

Key Findings / Current Evidence
After AI assistance, participants reported greater perceived sincerity and willingness to use AI support, alongside lower ownership and perspective-taking. Sincerity and ownership can move in different directions.

Interpretation
These preliminary patterns do not establish improved independent competence, lasting dependence, or a causal loss of agency.

What This Made Me Ask
I began to separate supported performance from independent capability. A more satisfactory expression is not the same outcome as a person becoming better able to interpret and respond to the next difficult situation.

Next Research Question
Test transfer directly: after AI support is withdrawn, do people use their own judgment, consider another person's perspective, and communicate differently in a subsequent human interaction? Repeated assessment could distinguish immediate assistance from learning over time.

Research Artifacts
