Xuechu Zhang

Human–AI Interaction · Communication · Social Dynamics

I study how artificial social actors reshape human judgment, relationships, and communities.

I am a psychology researcher at the University of Glasgow working at the intersection of human–AI interaction, communication, and social behavior. My work asks when AI scaffolds human capacities and when it begins to substitute for them—and how its effects extend into human relationships and collective social life.
