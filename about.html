About

I am a psychology researcher at the University of Glasgow, where I am completing a BSc (Hons) in Psychology, expected in 2027. I study how artificial social actors reshape human judgment, relationships, and communities.

My experience spans experimental design, quantitative and qualitative analysis, conversational data, multimodal measurement, and evidence synthesis. With Professor Angel Hsing-Chi Hwang of USC, I work on AI-assisted interpersonal communication. With Dr. Chen Zhou at Glasgow, I am developing a study of shared virtual humans and subsequent human group dynamics.

At the University of Hong Kong, I worked with Professor Cecilia Cheng's CARE Lab on a gaming disorder umbrella review and conducted an independent study of digital diaspora. Previously, I supported Professor Rachael Jack's lab research on sincerity and moral intent. A 2026 summer research visit to the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign further connected my interests in psychology and communication.

Across these experiences, I focus on turning theoretical questions into observable evidence. I use R, reproducible workflows, and mixed methods, and want to understand how immediate AI effects develop across interactions and time.

Beyond Research

Outside academia, I enjoy exploring new cities, documenting everyday life, and spending time with my cat.
