About

Having lived and worked across the UK, Hong Kong, and the US, I am a psychology researcher at the University of Glasgow, completing my BSc (Hons) in Psychology in 2027. I study how artificial social actors reshape human judgment, relationships, and communities.

My research spans AI-assisted interpersonal communication with Professor Angel Hsing-Chi Hwang at USC, shared virtual humans and group dynamics with Dr. Chen Zhou at Glasgow, and digital behavior and psychological adjustment at HKU. I have also worked on multimodal social perception and completed a 2026 research visit at UIUC, where I learned from Professor Mike Yao. Across these projects, I use experiments, conversational data, mixed methods, and reproducible R workflows to understand how the effects of AI develop across interactions and over time.

Beyond Research

Outside academia, I enjoy exploring new cities, documenting everyday life, and spending time with my cat.
