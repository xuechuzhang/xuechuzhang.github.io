    Human-only handoff · illustrative task

    People meet. The agent is absent.

    1. First decision: after a brief neutral introduction, each person privately chooses how many of ten tokens to contribute. In a study, choices must be independently committed and the payment rule honored.
    2. Then discussion: a 12-minute human-only discussion about a newcomer welcome activity. Record how people ask questions, take up ideas, and express disagreement.
    3. Then measures: partner-specific shared-reality reports and character recognition. These are proposed research measures, not collected by this demo.

    Understand the cooperation task

    Move the three illustrative contributions. All inputs belong to this visitor; there are no other connected people and no actual payments.

    Rule: multiply total contributions by 1.5, divide equally among three people, and add each person's unspent tokens. This calculator is not an outcome measure.
